# Knowledge Commons, Teaching, and Replication
**Draft v0.1 — 2026-11-02.** Part of Solarpunk Living World.

## Mission
Communities learn to observe, grow, repair, teach, and replicate what works. The durable reward is transferable competence, reusable knowledge, and improved real-world conditions. XP is optional delight, never a workforce score.

## Loop
NEED → EXPLORE → LEARN → PRACTICE → CAPTURE EVIDENCE → VERIFY → DOCUMENT → TEACH → ADAPT ELSEWHERE → MEASURE OUTCOMES → SHARE.

## First-class knowledge objects
Observation (photos, audio, notes, device readings); lesson; practical assessment; skill claim; peer endorsement; protocol; build card; project recipe; field journal; experiment; anonymized dataset; community story; adaptation/replication report. All are versioned with author attribution, provenance, permissions, review state, licensing, and relevant physical-world links.

## Skills
Separate self-declared interest, novice practice, observed ability, endorsed skill, professional certification, and teaching availability. A participant should be able to tell 'who knows how', 'who wants to learn', 'who is qualified to supervise' and 'who is available now' without being required to reveal personal data. Real-world authorization is never inferred from XP.

Skill graph examples: field observation → units/data literacy → soil sampling → calibration → ESP32 sensors → network gateways → ecological analysis → research design → teaching; or seed saving → propagation → nursery practice → transplanting → crop records → regenerative growing → mentoring.

## Portable learning records
Endorsements state issuer, assessed activity, evidence links, limits, expiry/revocation, and selective sharing policy. Each receiving community decides whether to recognize them. Achievements remain exportable in open formats and usable without this game.

## Shared knowledge and replication
A tested moisture sensor design becomes a reusable 'blueprint seed': bill of materials, salvaged alternatives, wiring, firmware, calibration procedure, troubleshooting, provenance, license and field performance. Another community can fork, adapt and log outcomes under its own conditions. Track successful independent replications, known failure modes, and measurement uncertainty.

## Living-world interactions
A library contains community-authored lessons; a repair shop tells a device's history; mentor lanterns signal opt-in teaching availability; skill constellations show pathways; an observatory lets people examine local field evidence; a community mural depicts verifiable shared outcomes. Failure is a learning artifact, not a penalty.

## Participatory governance
Community stewards control publication and moderation. Authors consent to public sharing; cultural knowledge and sensitive ecological coordinates have additional restrictions. No global popularity ratings, compulsory logging, or publication of individual productivity. Accessible text and offline/manual recording are equal interfaces.

## Acceptance criteria
A novice photographs a crop issue, gets AI suggestions with uncertainty, edits them, obtains a mentor's review, records an intervention, and later publishes a documented learning card. Another community can replicate a method without gaining access to confidential images or locations.
